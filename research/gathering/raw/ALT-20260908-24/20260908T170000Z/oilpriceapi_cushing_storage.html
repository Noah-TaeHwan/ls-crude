Why Cushing Storage Matters

WTI Price Discovery

Cushing is the physical delivery point for West Texas Intermediate (WTI) crude oil futures traded on NYMEX. Storage levels directly impact the futures curve structure and can cause significant price movements.

Trading Signals

  • High inventory: Often bearish for WTI prices
  • Low inventory: Often bullish, supporting backwardation
  • Near capacity: Can cause contango steepening

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the current Cushing oil storage level?

Current Cushing crude oil storage is 22.5 million barrels, representing approximately 29.6% of total capacity. Data is sourced from the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) weekly petroleum status report.

Why is Cushing storage important for oil prices?

Cushing, Oklahoma is the delivery point for WTI crude oil futures contracts traded on NYMEX. Storage levels at Cushing directly impact the WTI futures curve and can cause significant price movements when inventories approach capacity limits or draw down rapidly.

How often is Cushing storage data updated?

The EIA releases weekly petroleum status reports every Wednesday at 10:30 AM ET, which includes Cushing storage data. Our API updates with this data shortly after release.

What is the total storage capacity at Cushing?

Cushing has approximately 76 million barrels of working storage capacity across multiple tank farms. The hub is the largest crude oil storage facility in the United States and a critical piece of North American oil infrastructure.
