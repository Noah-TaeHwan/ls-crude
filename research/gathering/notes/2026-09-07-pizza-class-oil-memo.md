## 1. Four CAN paths and why each usually FAILS

**A. Demand-visibility path.** Local activity (truck-stop food, deliveries near plants) proxies real fuel use. CAN: distillate/freight aggregates co-move with goods movement — this is the honest core of ALT-20260907-02/03. FAIL: the direct fuel-demand test already died — 039 gasoline demand surprise REJECTED at IS r≈−0.005 / OOS r≈−0.009 (repo empirical only). The proxy-vs-thing gap kills most variants: BTS air passengers are not crew-changes (066 REJECTED), rental fleet counts are not fueling behavior (071 REJECTED), Top-5 menu rank is not quantity sold (070 HOLD; korea-pizza audit 2026-09-07). Nearest panel for late-night demand, 소상공인365, measures neither late-night nor industrial zones: https://www.data.go.kr/data/15143517/fileData.do?recommendDataYn=Y

**B. Supply-disruption path.** Chokepoint transits / refinery ops anticipate physical tightness. CAN: Hormuz tanker counts are genuine physical observation via IMF PortWatch: https://portwatch.imf.org/pages/data-and-methodology — the basis of ALT-20260907-04. FAIL: history starts 2019, so no full 2015–2023 IS exists (partial-sample only, no OOS claim allowed). Per-vessel AIS tracking is out of bounds and 017/023 died on it; 028 was killed on a false premise about which refinery exists where (repo empirical only). Estimates with retroactive revision are not as-of-safe without a vintage (testing-protocol §5; 051 HOLD).

**C. Positioning/crowding path.** Speculative extremes precede liquidation volatility. CAN: CFTC disaggregated COT is free, weekly, public — code 067651 for NYMEX WTI: https://www.cftc.gov/MarketReports/CommitmentsofTraders/index.htm — basis of ALT-20260907-01. FAIL: it is price-derived second-order signal, not physical activity, so the pizza analogy is thinnest here. Repo base rate on sign stability is grim: 045 americano IS +0.512 → OOS −0.511, 027 transit IS −0.332 → OOS +0.530, 040 SPR IS +0.051 → OOS −0.455 (all repo empirical only). Only a pre-frozen rule with full lag-curve disclosure plus a placebo (e.g. +26-week shift) is admissible.

**D. Narrative/attention path.** Search/news spikes front-run volatility. CAN: 052 household-panic Wiki is the sole WATCH (+0.135/+0.323, n=32 OOS months — repo empirical only). FAIL: 049/050 attention baskets are strong OOS-only with IS≈0 (+0.023/+0.422 and +0.023/+0.350) — textbook regime artifact, REJECTED/MONITOR-ONLY; 058 headline repetition dies after volume control (+0.091/+0.061); 054 is MEME DISCOVERY with max IS r=+0.081 over 62 assets (all repo empirical only). Attention without a physical anchor is the fastest route to an OOS-only mirage.

**Cross-cutting FAILs (apply to every row below):** calendar seasonality is not alpha (075 REJECTED AS ALPHA); service-sector growth confounds raw activity counts; monthly inputs have ~75-day publication-lag contracts and must never be forward-filled (ALT-20260907-03's BTS/FRED lag contract; FRED series https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=TSIFRGHT); full-sample standardisation and corpus TF-IDF are look-ahead (055 HOLD); anything touching 2024+ after exposure is exploratory, never E4 (testing-protocol §3).
