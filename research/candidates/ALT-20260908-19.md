## Ideas tested

1. KUSH/NOAA weather as a local operating-condition input;
2. KUSH After Dark event frequency as a local event-load input;
3. Google Maps Popular times / delivery availability for six Cushing quick-service restaurants;
4. fine-dining revenue or delivery-food demand as a labor/bonus proxy.

## Workflow result

Actual public samples and individual charts are in [091-Q/R/S](../indexes/091-cushing-operations-nowcasting/20260908T091QRSZ/README.md).

- NOAA KCUH weather passes as a measured **weather** context only.
- KUSH After Dark fails the intended-observation gate: only 4 of 31 returned entries mention Cushing and events lack a stable structured occurrence date.
- Maps displays relative popularity features for all six venues, but has no public reproducible historic busyness/order series. It is eligible only for future manual, fixed-time snapshots.
- Fine-dining sales, order volume, profits, bonuses, customers and delivery volume have no public aggregate long panel.

**Decision: PARK / E1.** No WTI/EIA association, score, composite, or ML model is run because none supplies an independently validated current-Cushing-activity target.
