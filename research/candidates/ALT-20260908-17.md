# ALT-20260908-17 — Cushing community footprint scope

## Hypothesis

Official Cushing High School enrollment may provide a long-run, aggregate community-footprint observation relevant to interpreting other CFAM local sources.

## Workflow result

The Oklahoma SDE annual public enrollment workbooks are accessible and five Cushing High School totals were extracted. The individual chart is in [091-P](../indexes/091-cushing-operations-nowcasting/20260908T091PZ/README.md). It is annual, subject to a missing extracted 2023-24 observation, and does not observe current activity, Cushing hub operations, people moving, or energy demand.

**Decision: PARK / E1.** Keep it as structural context only. A Cushing-specific, independent operational ground truth with a compatible multi-year cadence would be required before a measurement-validity test.
