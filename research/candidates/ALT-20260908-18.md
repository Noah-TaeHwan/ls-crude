# ALT-20260908-18 — KUSH local operational-attention scope

## Hypothesis

KUSH Radio’s public local-news chronology may yield a reproducible aggregate count of Cushing operational attention.

## Workflow result

The public WordPress metadata endpoint was retrieved. A fixed, high-precision title rule (`Cushing` plus a pre-registered operational keyword) produced a monthly aggregate visualised in [091-P](../indexes/091-cushing-operations-nowcasting/20260908T091PZ/README.md). The count is a record of editorial attention, not city activity, workers, traffic, inventory, or terminal throughput.

**Decision: PARK / E1.** No association test or CFAM score is warranted absent a compatible independent ground-truth busy-ness series. Raw post metadata is not committed; only hashes and aggregated counts are retained.
