## 2. Actual public sample

The public Census LODES8 Oklahoma 2022 OD main file was retrieved and its receipt is preserved at [`ALT-20260908-09`](../gathering/raw/ALT-20260908-09/20260908T034000Z/README.md). It contains 1,400,695 origin-destination rows. A reproducible coarse audit of workplace GEOIDs beginning `40119` found 27,056 OD rows and 32,543 jobs for Payne County.

This proves public aggregate employment-OD access. It does **not** make a Cushing field-activity or footfall series: the geography is county/block, the frequency is annual, and the data describe employment relationships rather than observed trips.

## 3. Workflow gate

| stage | result |
| --- | --- |
| data accessible? | **Yes, partly.** Public LODES annual aggregate OD file retrieved. |
| intended observation possible? | **No.** It cannot observe day/night activity, parking turnover, dwell, maintenance-day spikes, or individual site access. |
| individual visualization / target validation | **Not run.** One annual structural reference cannot be treated as a movement time series. |
| combination | **Not allowed.** Do not average annual LODES with monthly hotel tax or daily/weekly traffic. |

## 4. Decision

**PARK / E1.** LODES may later serve as a slow structural geography check after a true local aggregate movement series exists. It is not a substitute for phone mobility data.

Restart only after a fixed Cushing-area traffic counter or legally supplied aggregate mobility zone produces 60+ months with observation and publication timestamps. Then validate that input against LODES only at an annual aggregation, never row-by-row or person-by-person.
