## 3. Decision

**PARK / E1.** Do not build a seven-component score. The current actual inputs have unlike frequency and construct; most requested signals have no public long series. The only defensible future event study is pre-specified official notice dates versus a separately collected, fixed road-counter series.

No property-level parking, food-venue, hotel, or person/device observation will be collected. A manual count would be permissible only as a forward-looking, fixed public-right-of-way aggregate protocol with no photos, identifiers, plates, or retention of individual trajectories.
