# ALT-20260908-15 — Cushing Airport Activity Scope

## Hypothesis

The Cushing Municipal Airport monthly report may offer a small, independently measured local-activity panel: aviation-fuel gallons, transient overnight ramp stays/hangar rentals, emergency-flight support, and based aircraft. It could describe **airport activity**, which is closer to actual physical local activity than a city webpage, a job posting or nightlight.

It does not measure Cushing crude-terminal throughput, pipeline nominations, hotel occupancy, road trucking or the town’s total activity.

## Actual public sample

Four City Manager Reports dated 2023-05-15, 2023-06-20, 2023-07-17 and 2023-09-18 have a Cushing Regional Airport “Monthly report” section. The original PDFs, exact URLs, sizes, hashes and extracted values are in the [raw receipt](../gathering/raw/ALT-20260908-15/20260908T150000Z/README.md).

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |

The metrics are genuine monthly-report fields. Their observation windows are not stated separately in every report, so assigning them to the preceding calendar month would be an inference; the report date remains the only `available_at` field at this stage.

## Workflow gates

| gate | result |
| --- | --- |
| public data accessible? | **Yes.** Actual official reports and four numerical samples were obtained. |
| intended observation possible? | **Partial / yes for airport activity.** Fuel sales and ramp stays observe activity at this one airport, not Cushing’s oil hub. |
| long comparable history + individual availability date? | **No.** Four reports, one missing calendar month, and no verified 60-month archive. |
| individual visualization / association test | **NOT_RUN.** Four irregular observations cannot support a time-series test or a meaningful chart. |
| CFAM busy score | **No.** It may later be a separate airport component only after measurement-validity testing. |

## Decision

**PARK / E1 — strongest new municipal observation discovered, but not yet a usable panel.** The next gate is to retrieve at least 60 reports with the same metric definitions and preserve both report date and stated metric period. Then:

1. plot Jet-A, AvGas, ramp stays and emergency-flight support separately;
2. check missing reports/definition changes rather than filling gaps;
3. validate each against an independent local operational reference before considering any combination with hotel tax or a fixed truck count.

No WTI/EIA correlation or CFAM score was run. Four values must not be stretched into an oil result.
