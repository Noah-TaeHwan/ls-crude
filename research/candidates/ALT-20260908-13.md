## Hypothesis

An unusual rise in publicly listed City of Cushing vacancies could describe municipal staffing pressure. It does **not** directly measure Cushing terminal, pipeline, oil-worker, truck, lodging, refinery, or private-sector activity.

## Actual public sample

The official City Open Positions page was collected as a dated snapshot. It listed three positions:

1. Water Sewer Maintenance Skilled Laborer
2. Streets Skilled Laborer
3. Police Officer

The original HTML and checksum are recorded in the [raw receipt](../gathering/raw/ALT-20260908-13/20260908T050000Z/README.md). No applicant, employee, resident or recruiter data were collected.

| individual visualization / association test | **NOT_RUN.** One 3-listing snapshot cannot be made into a time series. |

## Decision

**PARK / E1.** Start a forward-only public snapshot archive if the page’s terms permit it: retrieve at a fixed weekly cadence, store only listing title/URL/count and retrieval time, retain postings/closures as page-state changes, and do not infer applicant counts or individual behavior. After at least 60 monthly observations—and only alongside an independent operating reference such as a fixed truck count—test whether municipal-vacancy pressure is a separate local-services observation. It is not an oil indicator by itself.
