## 3. Individual visualization and test status

![Exact-query monthly source sample, 2015–2023](../indexes/091-cushing-operations-nowcasting/20260908T091HGZ/figures/091h-cushing-travel-search-sample.svg)

**NOT_RUN for activity, EIA, or price.** The chart confirms that a public long history exists, but it also shows a sparse exact phrase. A zero can mean very low normalized interest, not zero travelers. The index has no visit purpose, origin/destination, lodging, employer, or job/terminal attribute.

It would be a methodological error to:

- substitute generic `Cushing, Oklahoma` interest (that changes the construct to place/news attention);
- add phrases after viewing results and average their incomparable query-normalized scales;
- interpret an apparent rise as oil-field work rather than local travel, education, family, services, news, or navigation;
- jump to EIA or WTI testing before validating that this search proxy measures visits or operations.
