## Question

Can US alternative-energy policy make Cushing operationally busier or quieter, and can a public series validate that claim?

## Causal audit

```text
policy enactment / implementation
  → technology adoption, electricity and transport-fuel substitution
  → national / regional crude-demand and refinery-flow changes
  → pipeline nominations, storage flows or terminal work at Cushing
  → local activity (trucks, contractors, lodging, shifts)
```

Every arrow is slow, confounded and can have either sign. A renewable-electricity policy can displace mainly coal/gas rather than oil; EV policy can reduce transport fuel demand but does not identify a particular Cushing inflow/outflow or local work shift.

## Existing public evidence and collection status

| input | actual source already in repository | what it measures | CFAM suitability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EV oil displacement / EV sales | Factor [004](../factors/004-renewable-displacement/README.md), IEA Global EV Data Explorer public collection | annual technology adoption and estimated oil displacement | structural context only |
| renewable generation | 004A screened it out | electricity generation, mainly coal/gas substitution path | not an oil/Cushing activity input |
| policy events / subsidies | no single long, as-of, national exposure panel collected | heterogeneous laws, implementation and litigation | not collected |
| Cushing busy ground truth | none | fixed truck flow, operations, lodging or throughput would be required | unavailable |

The actual 004 IEA sample has only 7–8 usable annual 2015–2023 comparisons. Its descriptive next-year WTI relationships (`r=-.280` displacement level, `r=-.230` change) were explicitly held as insufficient. It is not a policy-vintage panel and has no Cushing activity label.

## Workflow result

| gate | verdict |
| --- | --- |
| data exists? | **Partial.** Annual adoption outcome data exist; a stable policy-exposure/vintage panel is not collected. |
| does it observe Cushing busy? | **No.** It does not observe terminal throughput, staffing, traffic, pipeline nominations, lodging or maintenance. |
| individual visualization / statistical validation | **NOT_RUN.** Annual n≤8 and no ground truth make correlation, event study, ML, DiD or causal claims invalid for CFAM. |
| combine into busy score? | **No.** It would mix a slow national structural variable with local operational state. |

## Decision

**PARK / E1.** Keep US alternative-energy policy as a long-run scenario/context note under Factor 004, not a CFAM input. Reopen only when both conditions hold:

1. A dated, versioned policy-exposure panel is fixed *before* analysis; and
2. at least 60 months of independent Cushing operational ground truth exists.

The first valid test would be a pre-specified lagged structural/event analysis against that operational target—not a WTI proxy, not a retroactive policy-count index, and not a 0–100 busy score.
