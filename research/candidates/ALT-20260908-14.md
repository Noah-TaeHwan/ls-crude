## Question

Does the City of Cushing website expose long, dated, aggregate records that can help answer the narrower CFAM question: **is Cushing locally busier than its own normal level?**

This is a source audit, not a claim that the city site already produces an activity index.

## What is worth pursuing next

1. Search the agenda packets for a **pre-existing monthly aggregate** of permits/inspections, airport operations/fuel sales, utility load, street/fleet work orders or water throughput.
2. Before extraction, require at least 60 months, a stable definition, the underlying month, the packet publication date and an independent operating reference.
3. Plot that series alone first. Only a measurement-valid series may be considered alongside hotel tax or a fixed truck counter.

## Decision

**PARK / E1 — source inventory passed; no new quantitative input passed.** The city website is valuable because it reveals where official administrative records may live, especially dated agenda attachments. It does not justify treating municipal web traffic, job ads, page updates, meetings or events as Cushing oil-hub activity.
