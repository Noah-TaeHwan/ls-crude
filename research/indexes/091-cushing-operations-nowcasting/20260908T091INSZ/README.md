## What the re-audit changes

- I, M and L now have descriptive visualisations of their actual collected samples.
- J, K and N now have explicit gateboard visualisations rather than unillustrated prose. A `0` means no lawful/repeatable measurement exists; it is not a zero-activity observation.
- O, P and Q/R/S already had their individual figures; they are referenced here rather than duplicated.
- No new WTI/EIA test is run: none of I–N provides an independent current-Cushing-activity target with a comparable timestamped panel.

## Current best candidates for “is Cushing busy now?”

1. **091-S — Google Maps Quick-Service Live Footfall Panel**: closest accessible real-time city-footfall observation; six fixed venues, forward-only, manual public UI log. It must pass its [90-day protocol](../20260908T091QRSZ/091s-live-monitor-protocol.md).
2. **091-O — municipal airport Jet-A/AvGas, ramp stays and emergency-flight support**: strongest already obtained *numeric physical-activity* observation. It needs a continuous report history and explicit metric-period timestamps.
3. **091-A — City hotel/motel tax**: broad external-worker/overnight-stay context, but monthly and lagged; useful only once the long dated raw panel is recovered.
4. **fixed Cushing-area truck counter (not yet obtained)**: conceptually the best direct work/logistics measure, but currently no fixed long public panel has passed the data gate.

KUSH operational attention, weather, events, school enrollment, municipal vacancies, sales/use tax, LODES, and nightlights remain context or audit variables—not top busy-now indicators.
