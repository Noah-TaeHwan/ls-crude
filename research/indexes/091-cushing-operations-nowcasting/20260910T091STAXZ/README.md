# 091-STAX — Cushing city monthly sales-tax distributions (2026-09-10)

**What this is:** City of Cushing, Oklahoma monthly **sales-tax collections**
as distributed by the Oklahoma Tax Commission (OTC), from the keyless monthly
Sales Tax News Release tables (`STS-Current.pdf` = September 2026,
`STS-Past1.pdf` = August 2026). Each release prints the distribution month
plus the same month of the prior year, giving 4 dated city points. US dollars
as printed.

**What this is not:** use tax (STU untouched), hotel/lodging tax (the failed
pilots stay out), Payne County or statewide totals, busy, or WTI. Periods are
OTC **distribution** months; OTC notes September 2026 primarily represents
July business and August 2026 primarily June business — documented here, not
relabeled. Missing months (OTC keeps only two releases online) stay missing;
no 0-fill.
