## What it can help with

Use the site to discover a dated candidate event under a frozen list:

`Cushing`, `inventory`, `pipeline`, `apportionment`, `maintenance`,
`turnaround`, `outage`, `terminal`, `storage`, `rail`, `refinery`.

For each match, retain only URL, title, publisher timestamp, collection
timestamp, matched terms, and a human label of **context only** or **candidate
for independent confirmation**. Confirm numerical claims against EIA, an
operator, regulator, or another primary source before use elsewhere.

## What it cannot establish

- It cannot provide a daily Cushing throughput, tank fill, truck count, or
  capacity-utilization series.
- It cannot be counted as an independent confirmation when it quotes EIA,
  Bloomberg, OilPrice, API, or a named primary release.
- Article volume or a dramatic headline is not a CFAM busy score.
- No article body is copied, scraped in bulk, or stored as a research dataset.

## Collection protocol

One fixed-time forward review each business day; deduplicate by canonical URL;
record corrections/updated timestamps; do not backfill values from later edits.
After 90 days, assess only (1) whether the source finds independently verified
Cushing-relevant events earlier than the existing KUSH/Google News queue and
(2) whether labels are reproducible between reviewers. This is a source-quality
test, not an oil-price backtest.
